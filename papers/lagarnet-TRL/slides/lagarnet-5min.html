LaGarNet

Latent world models for quasi-static pick-and-place garment flattening

A single, simulation-trained policy successfully flattens four distinct garment types in the real world without further fine-tuning—achieving zero-shot transfer.

The task

Flattening a crumpled garment

  • Necessary precursor to downstream tasks (folding, ironing, pressing) [Doumanoglou et al., 2016].
  • High degrees of freedom, severe non-linear deformations, and top-down self-occlusion.
  • Constrained physical setup (single arm, discrete primitive, one RGB camera) for representation-focused evaluation.
Simulation [Bertiche et al., 2020]
Real garments
Related work

Three families of data-driven flatteners

Behaviour cloning

Conceptually straightforward, but the vast state space of crumpled configurations demands an impractical volume of human demonstrations. Consequently, these methods often rely on scripted oracles or are restricted to a single garment type [Weng et al., 2022; Lips et al., 2024].

Model-free RL

Directly explores this state distribution in simulation, yielding the most general flatteners currently available. However, this comes at the cost of poor sample efficiency and an inability to predict the forward effects of actions [Canberk et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2024].

Model-based RL

Rarely applied to garments. Standard latent models often produce blurred reconstructions, losing the critical edge and corner features necessary for shaping. To date, no mesh-free planner has matched the performance of a mesh-based equivalent [Lin et al., 2022; Huang et al., 2022].

Research question

Can latent world models built for continuous control still function in quasi-static primitive setups, where consecutive observations are not temporally continuous?

Contribution 1

A goal-conditioned recurrent state-space model

  • Direct integration of goal-conditioning into latent inference for prior p(z̃ | h, g) and posterior q(ẑ | h, x, g).
  • Recurrent backbone for occluded state belief and dual mask/reward decoding.
  • Cross-entropy method for action sampling and reward-based ranking [Hafner et al., 2019].
  • Removing latent goal-conditioning costs 22.5% points of success rate (75.0% → 52.5%).
Contribution 2

Data collection without a scripted oracle

  • Diffusion Policy expert [Chi et al., 2023] trained on 50 human demonstrations for reaching near-flattened states.
  • Mask-biased random policy for uniform pick-point exploration.
  • 1:1 policy mixture yielding 5,000 episodes (100k transitions) without scripted oracles.
  • Cloth-agnostic paradigm for broader deformable object generalisation.

Contribution 3

A coverage-alignment reward

𝓡CA = α·ΔNC + (1−α)·ΔMaxIoU

α = 0.3  ·  both terms lie in [−1, 1], so 𝓡CA ∈ [−1, 1]

  • Joint evaluation of state coverage and alignment via top-down binary masks.
  • Elimination of privileged particle-level state [Canberk et al., 2023] and learned smoothness estimators [Avigal et al., 2022].
  • Continuous and linear formulation for predictive learning compatibility.
Sensitivity to the interpolation coefficient α
Results · simulation

Comparable to mesh-based planning

  • Parity with state-of-the-art mesh-based planner MEDOR [Huang et al., 2022].
  • Superiority of a single, unified policy over single-garment variants via morphological regularisation.
  • >95% normalised coverage across all garment categories within 20 steps.
Results · computational cost

Comparable quality at a deployable cost

LaGarNet requires five times fewer parameters than MEDOR and plans seventeen times faster, with no loss in flattening quality. Six seconds of planning per action is affordable on a physical robot where a hundred is not, though the full perception–action cycle (about 24.5 s per step) remains the deployment bottleneck.

Conclusion

Lessons and future work

  • Efficacy of latent world models in quasi-static regimes via direct latent goal-conditioning.
  • Superiority of greedy, one-step planning due to rapid predictive error compounding (73.3% at H=1 vs. 30.0% at H=5).
  • Absence of an explicit stopping criterion as the principal system limitation.
  • Future integration of learned termination conditions and extension to goal-directed folding.
Outlook

A compact latent space capable of encoding high-dimensional, deformable dynamics is not restricted to garments. This formulation provides a promising pathway toward general deformable-object manipulation—such as dough, clay, or cables—where explicit state representations remain computationally prohibitive.
